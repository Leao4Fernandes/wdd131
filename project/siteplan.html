RugbyHub

Website Site plan

Site Name

RugbyHub

RugbyHub is a website created for rugby fans and players who want to discover information about rugby teams, competitions, players, matches, and the sport itself.

Site Purpose

The purpose of RugbyHub is to provide a central resource for Rugby fans and players by providing information about teams, competitions, players, matches, and rugby rules.

Scenarios

  • What are the upcoming matches?
  • What are the basic rules of rugby?
  • Which teams participate in major rugby competitions?
