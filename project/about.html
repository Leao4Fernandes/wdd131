ABOUT SUPER XV

More than a game.

Super XV is a rugby competition that brings together teams, players and supporters from across Portugal. Our goal is to celebrate the sport and help rugby continue to grow in our communities.

01

Our Mission

To promote rugby in Portugal and create a competitive, respectful and exciting environment for players and clubs.

02

Competition

Bringing clubs together to compete throughout the season while creating memorable moments for players and supporters.

03

Community

Rugby is built around people. We want to connect clubs, players, coaches, families and supporters through the sport we love.

04

Development

Supporting the next generation of players and helping create new opportunities for rugby throughout Portugal.
